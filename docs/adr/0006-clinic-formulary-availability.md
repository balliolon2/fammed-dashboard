# 0006: Clinic Formulary Availability Filtering

Because primary care centers and community hospitals frequently lack tertiary neuropathic pain medications (such as Duloxetine, Oxcarbazepine, or Lidocaine patches), the system introduces a clinic-level formulary availability toggle. Out-of-stock drugs are visually flagged and de-prioritized in favor of in-stock first-line alternatives, preventing unfillable recommendations while maintaining guideline visibility.
