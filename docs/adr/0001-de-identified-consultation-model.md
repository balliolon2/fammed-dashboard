# 0001: De-identified Patient Consultation Model

To ensure strict compliance with the Thai Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and medical data regulations without requiring heavy hospital infrastructure, the system will not store direct personally identifiable information (such as patient names, national citizen IDs, or hospital numbers). Consultations are bound to de-identified Patient Cases identified by generated reference codes, demographic summaries (age range, sex), and physiological indicators (eGFR, LFT, comorbidities) necessary for clinical decision support.
